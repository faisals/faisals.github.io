Faisal Sajjad

Engineer. Thirteen years in production, now building with agents.

I've worked across most layers of production software — site reliability, performance, data, and product engineering — growing from software engineer to team lead without ever stopping shipping. I like owning a problem end to end: finding it, building the fix, and making sure it stays fixed.

Most of my building now happens with agents. The question I keep coming back to is the one reliability work trained me on: when systems do the work, how do you see what they're doing and know when to trust them?
